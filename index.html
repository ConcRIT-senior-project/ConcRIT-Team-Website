Department Inventory Resource Technology

Our capstone project brings the lab floor and the data layer together. From intake to final report, D.I.R.T. keeps the experiments being done visible, organized, and ready to invoice.

01 — The project

Every specimen has a story.
D.I.R.T. helps you keep it straight.

The team will design and develop a secure, role-based laboratory management system for Atlantic Testing Laboratories (ATL) that supports concurrent access for technicians, managers, and admins. This management system will allow: tracking of clients, projects, and the samples or specimens associated with them throughout the testing lifecycle; maintenance of an interoffice chain of custody record, showing where each sample or specimen currently resides; batch analysis and reporting of tests; retention and final disposition tracking of unused samples; and operational personalized dashboards based on the staffs’ role.
